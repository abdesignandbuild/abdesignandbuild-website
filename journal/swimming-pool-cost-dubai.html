How much does a swimming pool cost in Dubai?

A clear, honest guide to villa pool prices in Dubai for 2026: the typical ranges, what sits inside them, and the decisions that move the number up or down.

The short answer

For a private villa in Dubai, a new in-ground concrete pool typically starts from around AED 55,000 for a small plunge pool, AED 85,000 to 140,000 for a family pool, and AED 140,000 and upwards for a large or lap pool, excluding VAT. Natural stone, mosaic and bespoke features push those figures higher.

These are starting ranges, not quotes. Two pools of the same size can differ in price by fifty per cent or more, depending on access, ground conditions and finishes. The rest of this guide explains why.

What a pool price should include

When you compare quotes, check that each one covers the same things. A complete villa pool price normally includes:

  • Design and drawings, including the structural and pool-services drawings needed for approvals.
  • Excavation and removal of soil from site.
  • A reinforced concrete shell with proper waterproofing. This is the part you never see and the part you should never economise on.
  • Filtration and plant room: pump, filter, pipework, balance tank or skimmers, and the controls.
  • Interior finish: tiles, mosaic or a render finish, plus waterline detailing.
  • Coping and the immediate surround, usually in stone or porcelain.
  • Basic underwater lighting.
  • A child-safety barrier around the pool, which the authorities require for private pools.

If a quote looks much lower than the others, the difference is often in what has been left out: approvals, waterproofing, the plant room, or reinstating the garden after the excavator leaves.

What makes a pool cost more

Size and depth

More water means more concrete, more excavation and larger equipment. Depth matters too: a uniform depth of around 1.2 to 1.4 metres suits most families and keeps costs sensible, while a deep end adds structure and volume.

Access to the garden

Many Dubai villas have narrow side returns. If an excavator cannot reach the back garden, soil has to be moved by smaller machines or by hand, and concrete may need to be pumped over the house. Access is one of the biggest hidden cost drivers, and the reason a proper site visit matters.

Ground conditions

Near the coast and on reclaimed land, groundwater can sit close to the surface. Keeping an excavation dry during construction adds time and cost, so this needs to be understood before the price is fixed.

Finishes

Glass mosaic, hand-cut stone and full natural-stone decking cost considerably more than porcelain. They also change how the pool looks and feels. Pale stones such as travertine stay cooler underfoot in summer, which is worth paying for on the surfaces you actually walk on.

Features

  • A sun shelf with in-pool loungers
  • Built-in steps, benches or a swim-up seat
  • An infinity or overflow edge
  • Spa jets, a jacuzzi or a water feature
  • Colour-changing lighting and automation you control from your phone
  • Salt chlorination

Keeping the water cool

In July and August an outdoor pool in Dubai can become uncomfortably warm. A pool chiller (which can usually also heat the water in winter) is one of the most useful upgrades, and it is far simpler to plan at design stage than to add later. We cover this, and how shade helps, in Designing a pool for the Dubai summer.

Costs people forget

  • Approvals from your community developer and the relevant authority. Read our guide to approvals for pools and landscaping in Dubai communities.
  • Garden reinstatement: the planting, irrigation and paving around the pool once construction is finished.
  • Shade: a pergola or shade structure decides whether you can use the pool between May and October.
  • Running costs: electricity for the pump and chiller, water top-ups, chemicals and a regular maintenance visit.

How long does it take?

Most villa pools take around three to four months from approved design to handover. Allow time before that for design and approvals. Larger pools, complex finishes or difficult access add to the programme.

How to get the most from your budget

  1. Size the pool for how you will use it. If the pool is mainly for cooling off and lounging, a well-designed plunge pool with a sun shelf can give more pleasure than a larger, plainer pool.
  2. Protect the invisible parts. Structure, waterproofing and filtration are expensive to fix later. Save on features before you save on these.
  3. Spend on what you touch. Coping, steps and the deck you walk on barefoot deserve the best material you can afford.
  4. Design the pool and garden together. Shade, planting, lighting and paving decide how the pool feels. Planned as one, they cost less than when they are added piece by piece.
  5. Use one team from design to handover. Fewer handovers between companies means fewer gaps, fewer variations and a result that matches the drawing.

Common questions

Are Dubai pool prices quoted with or without VAT?

Most contractors quote excluding VAT, which is currently charged at 5% in the UAE. Always check, so that you compare quotes on the same basis.

Do I need approval to build a pool at my villa?

Yes. In most Dubai villa communities you need a no-objection certificate from the master developer and a permit from the relevant authority before work starts. We prepare the drawings and handle these approvals for our clients.

How long does it take to build a villa pool in Dubai?

Most villa pools take around three to four months from approved design to handover. Design and approvals come before that, and size, finishes and site access all affect the programme.

Can I fit a pool into a small garden?

Often, yes. A plunge pool of up to around 15 square metres, with a sun shelf or built-in bench, can work in a compact garden or courtyard. Access for construction is usually the deciding factor, so a site visit comes first.

Is a pool chiller worth it in Dubai?

For most families, yes. Without one, an outdoor pool can become too warm to be refreshing in high summer. Planning for a chiller at design stage is simpler and cheaper than adding one later.
