A&B was founded around a simple belief: the standard presented at the beginning of a project should be the standard delivered at the end.

Dubai-based softscape expert

Planting chosen to look beautiful on day one, and still be thriving in August

I believe a garden in Dubai has to earn its place. It should be beautiful, it should suit the way you live, and it has to survive the summer.

Hi, I'm Banu, co-founder of A&B and the person who will design your planting.

Plenty of plants look wonderful on the day they go in. The question I always ask is how they will look in August, after weeks above forty degrees. So I won't plant something just because it pleases on the day. I'll walk you through every choice, explain what each tree, palm and plant needs, and tell you honestly when something won't cope here.

Then I'm on site with our crew, setting out every plant myself, so the garden that grows is the one we agreed: one you'll enjoy for years, not just for a season.

From site to finish

A garden is decided long before the planting goes in. These are our team's own photographs, taken on site during the works and on completion.

  1. Protect & prepare

    Before any work starts, new paving, gates and finishes are sheeted and protected, and the existing trees are kept. Nothing that is already right gets damaged on the way to the result.

  2. Build & plant

    Mosaic is laid tile by tile, irrigation and lighting are hidden in the beds, then the softscaping: specimen palms, layered tropical planting and finished ground cover, set out to the design.

  3. Finish & hand over

    Edges are cleaned, planting is dressed and every light is checked after dark. You receive a garden that is finished, not one that needs another month to look right.

How long until you can enjoy your new home?

Every project is different, but here is what to expect for a typical villa pool and garden.

  1. Phase 1: Consultation

    1–2 weeks

    Your first call, the site visit and an agreed brief.

  2. Phase 2: Design

    2–4 weeks

    Concept, materials and layout, refined with you until it feels right, then a detailed proposal.

  3. Phase 3: Approvals

    2–4 weeks

    We prepare the drawings and handle community and authority approvals, while ordering long-lead materials.

  4. Phase 4: Build

    2–4 months

    Construction, planting and finishing, then a full walkthrough before handover. A garden alone is often quicker; a pool usually takes three to four months.

    Questions, answered

    Can't see yours? WhatsApp us.

    Is the first consultation free?

    Yes. The first consultation is free and without obligation. We talk through your property, what you'd like to change and your timeline, and tell you honestly whether we're the right team for it.

    Do you only work on gardens and pools?

    No. We work across interiors, exteriors, renovation and construction. Many clients start with a pool or garden and bring the inside of the house into the same project, so one team holds the whole design together.

    How long does a pool or garden take?

    Most villa pools take around three to four months from approved design to handover. A garden without a pool is usually quicker. Size, finishes and community approvals all affect the programme, and your proposal sets out the timeline in full.

    Do you handle community and authority approvals?

    Yes. We prepare the drawings and documents your community or the authorities need, and coordinate the approval process with you before work begins.

    How much will my project cost?

    It depends on the size, the scope and the level of finish. Our cost guide gives an indicative starting range in a minute. Your exact figure comes in a detailed proposal after the site visit.

    Can we stay in the house while you work?

    Usually, yes. We plan access, protection and working hours around your household, and keep the site clean and secure at the end of each day.

    Can you build a design from another designer or architect?

    Yes. We can build to drawings you already have, refine them with you, or design the whole project ourselves. Either way, we hold the build to the design that was agreed.
